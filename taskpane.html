Reply Polisher

Settings

Create one at console.x.ai (API Keys). A SuperGrok / X Premium subscription does not include API access.

Your key is saved in your Outlook mailbox settings for this add-in (or this browser if those are unavailable). Email text you polish is sent to the AI provider above.

Write your reply as usual, then click the button. Your draft and the earlier email are sent to the AI and a cleaned-up version appears below. Nothing changes until you click Replace my draft.

Get a suggested reply to this email. Optionally jot down what you want to say.

Replace keeps your signature and the earlier emails below your reply when Outlook exposes them. Use Ctrl+Z / Cmd+Z in the message to undo.

Open this add-in while writing a reply (or while reading an email) in Outlook.
